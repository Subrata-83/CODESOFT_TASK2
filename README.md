# CODESOFT_TASK4

## Customer Data Analysis

The project focuses on analyzing customer data to understand **customer behavior, purchasing patterns, customer segments, and business performance** using Python.

### What This Project Does

- Cleans and preprocesses customer data
- Handles missing values and duplicate records
- Analyzes customer age, income, purchases, loyalty, and spending
- Performs customer segmentation
- Identifies high-value and potential customers
- Analyzes sales by product category, location, loyalty status, and purchase channel
- Creates charts and visual reports
- Generates business insights and marketing recommendations

### Customer Segmentation

Customers are divided into four groups:

- **High Value** – High spending and frequent purchases
- **Loyal** – Gold/Platinum customers with frequent purchases
- **Potential** – Customers with good spending and purchase frequency
- **Low Engagement** – Customers with lower engagement
